The Battle of Singapore lasted from 8 to 15 February 1942. Although the Allied garrison was numerically larger, it included exhausted troops from Malaya and inexperienced reinforcements. A headcount alone does not establish an army's fighting strength.

Japanese troops crossed the Johor Straits and landed on Singapore's northwestern coast on 8 February. They advanced towards Bukit Timah, an area containing important fuel and supply depots. Misunderstood orders contributed to an Allied withdrawal from the Kranji-Jurong defence line. Japanese forces captured Bukit Timah on 11 February.

Allied resistance continued. The Malay Regiment fought strongly at Pasir Panjang on 13–14 February, before being overwhelmed.

Source limitation: This later overview connects geography, supplies and fighting. It does not establish that defenders lacked courage, or that any single mistake explains the whole defeat. The wording above is a modern paraphrase.
