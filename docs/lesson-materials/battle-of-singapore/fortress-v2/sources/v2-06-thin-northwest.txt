SOURCE V2-06: Where the defenders were thin

Type: (c) Prepared summary of two later heritage and museum accounts, with short quoted phrases.
Prepared: 11 November 2026.
Based on: National Heritage Board, World War II Trail booklet (Roots.gov.sg), sections on the invasion, Sarimbun and Pulau Ubin; Chris Coulthard-Clark, 1942: Fall of Singapore, Australian War Memorial, talk of 15 February 2002.
Reference URL: https://www.roots.gov.sg/-/media/Roots/Files/world-war-2/world-war-2-trail-booklet.pdf?la=en
Reference URL: https://www.awm.gov.au/articles/blog/1942-fall-of-singapore

The National Heritage Board booklet says Percival "believed that the Japanese would invade Singapore from the east", so he put more troops in the north-east, while "the north-western half was assigned relatively fewer troops." Japanese field intelligence then found "the lack of depth in the defences of north-western Singapore".

The 22nd Australian Brigade had three battalions to cover the coast from Sungei Kranji to Sungei Berih. They were overstretched, and the booklet says that at the landing on 8 February "Japanese forces enjoyed a seven to one numerical advantage against the Australians."

The day before, 400 Japanese soldiers had landed on Pulau Ubin, off the north-east coast: "a crafty move to distract the defending forces from the real invasion in the north-west", with artillery fire "to further reinforce the bluff."

The Australian War Memorial talk adds that a fresh formation, the 18th British Division, was available, but Percival gave the most exposed north-western sector to Bennett's two brigades, "now reduced to half-strength".

Source limitation: These are later public-history accounts without detailed footnotes. They conflict with Percival's own despatch (Source V2-05), which says he expected the attack from the west, although his despatch's list of forces also puts two divisions in the north. "seven to one" describes the landing area, not the whole island. Finest Hour (2015) dates the Pulau Ubin diversion to 5 February; the booklet says 7 February.
