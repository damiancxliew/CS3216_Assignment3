11 February. A letter from Yamashita "was dropped from an aeroplane calling upon me to surrender the fortress." Percival did not reply.

14 February. The Supreme Commander (Wavell) to Percival: "Your gallant stand is serving purpose and must be continued to limit of endurance."

15 February, morning. Wavell: "When you are fully satisfied that this is no longer possible I give you discretion to cease resistance."

15 February, conference at Fort Canning. Percival saw two choices: "to counter-attack immediately to regain control of the reservoirs and of the military food depots" or "to capitulate immediately." He wrote: "I could see no immediate solution for the critical water situation and decided to capitulate."

Percival's last telegram to Wavell: "Owing to losses from enemy action, water, petrol, food, and ammunition practically finished. Unable therefore to continue the fight any longer."

Source limitation: The telegrams are quoted inside later texts, not seen in the originals. Percival says the conference "concurred unanimously"; BiblioAsia (Source D) records reluctance and a rejected counter-attack. Hosts disagree on the signing time: the heritage booklet says 6.10pm, SG101 says 7.50pm.
