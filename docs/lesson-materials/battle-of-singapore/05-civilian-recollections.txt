SOURCE E: Confidence and the experience of air raids

Type: Short extracts from later oral-history interviews, reproduced in a National Archives article, with prepared connecting text.
Prepared: 28 October 2026.
Published selection: WWII Comes to Singapore, National Archives of Singapore.
Reference: https://corporate.nas.gov.sg/discover-archives/media/ww2comestosingapore/

Rudy William Mosbergen recalled the first air raid and the timing of its warning:

"But the air raid sirens were sounding when the Japanese bombers had left."

Interview recorded in 2006; accession 003051/1.

Ronald Hubert Barth recalled confidence in the island's defence, including the belief that:

"Singapore was an impregnable fortress"

Interview recorded in 1984; accession 000394/5.

The archive selection also describes civil-defence workers assisting people wounded in air raids. Military defeat affected civilians before the surrender itself.

Source limitation: These witnesses were interviewed decades afterwards. Their recollections provide particular experiences and remembered expectations, not a survey of all residents or a complete assessment of military readiness. The short quotations were checked against the published archive selection; the full recordings were not reviewed for this pack.
