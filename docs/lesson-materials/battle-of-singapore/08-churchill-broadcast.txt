SOURCE H: Churchill's broadcast on the fall of Singapore

Type: Short quoted extract from a later article, which quotes part of a wartime broadcast. Not a recording or full transcript.
Prepared: 11 November 2026.
Based on: Fred Glueckstein, Churchill and the Fall of Singapore, Finest Hour 169, Summer 2015, International Churchill Society.
Reference: https://winstonchurchill.org/?p=6007

The article, after describing the surrender of 15 February 1942, says:

That day, Churchill broadcast the tragic news about Singapore to the British people and the world. The Prime Minister said in part: "I speak to you all under the shadow of a heavy and far-reaching military defeat. It is a British and Imperial defeat. Singapore has fallen."

The article cites Martin Gilbert, Churchill: The Power of Words (2012), p. 312.

Source limitation: The wording was checked on 11 November 2026 against this published article, not against a recording or transcript of the broadcast; curly quotation marks are written here as straight ones, and the article's closing ellipsis is omitted. It is the view of Britain's wartime Prime Minister, speaking to the public in Britain, not an account of events in Singapore.
