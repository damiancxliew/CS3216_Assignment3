SOURCE D: What the commanders discussed on 15 February

Type: Prepared summary of a later article citing participants' records; includes one short quoted diary passage.
Prepared: 28 October 2026.
Based on: Phan Ming Yen, The Other Men Who Surrendered Singapore, BiblioAsia, National Library Board, 1 July 2024.
Reference: https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-20-issue-2-jul-sep-2024-men-surrender-singapore-japanese-occupation/

On 15 February, Percival met eleven other men at the Battlebox. Reports concerned water, ammunition, food and the worsening civilian situation. The article describes Heath as urging surrender, Bennett as rejecting a proposed counterattack, and Simmons as reluctant but unable to see an alternative.

The article reproduces Brigadier Eric Goodman's diary description of the conference:

"it was useless to continue the fight as water and ammunition were failing"

Percival remained responsible for the decision, but the consultation involved others. Surrender was subsequently negotiated at the Ford Factory, not at the Battlebox.

Source limitation: The quotation was checked against this published article, not the original diary. Commanders' accounts illuminate their assessments; they do not prove that no earlier alternative existed.
