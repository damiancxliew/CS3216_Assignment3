# PRD v3: Glossary

> **Derived view, do not edit.** Generated from [`../PRD-v3.md`](../PRD-v3.md) and

## Structure
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Adventure** | One generated, playable episode built from a teacher's materials plus the wider historical record | G1, G1.1 |
| **Stage** | One part of an adventure, ending in a defining moment. 1–2 preferred, 3 only for simple stages | G2, G2.2 |
| **Central question** | The stage's dilemma, always visible to the student | G5.2, P7 |
| **Session** | One play-through: briefing, stages, epilogue, Interview, reveal. Defaults to 45 minutes | P1.4, P1.5, C1 |

## The player
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Who the student plays. Tentatively a fictional go-between trusted to different degrees by several sides | G3–G3.6 |
| **Private brief** | The role's goals, fears, constraints and what they know at that moment | G3.2 |
| **Go-between act** | One of 2–4 written choices at a defining moment: what the student advises, conveys or keeps back | P9.1 |
| **Deception act** | A written go-between act that misrepresents something, where the episode suits it | P9.13 |

## Characters
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Major character** | 2–4 per stage; full persona, stances, triggers and several discoveries | G8 |
| **Minor character** | 1–3 per stage; light persona, 1–2 discoveries, no stances | G8.1 |
| **Persona** | Voice and real phrases, what they knew then, what they want from the student, what they slant or withhold | G7, P11–P11.4 |
| **Stance** | One of 3–5 character-specific attitudes (for example guarded, wary, alarmed, confiding). Shown through the portrait's expression and a subtle notice, never by name | G4, P6–P6.5 |
| **Hidden top stance** | A stance reachable only through good play; can unlock a secret ending | G6.1.2 |
| **Trigger** | A written rule: a student action → a stance change → what it unlocks or closes | G4.1, P6 |
| **Trust trigger** | Every major character's built-in trigger: two challenged claims lower their stance by one | G4.3, P8.4 |
| **Slant** | A written, deliberately one-sided claim a character makes; some documents contradict it | G10.4, P13.5 |
| **Summons** | A character sends for the student at a story event | P12 |

## Knowledge
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | A written, grounded piece of understanding a character can reveal when its condition is met | G5, P7.1 |
| **Reveal condition** | What must be true before a discovery can be revealed: earlier discoveries and/or a stance | G5 |
| **Core discovery** | A discovery essential to understanding the episode, held only by a major character or a document. Hints steer towards it; the reveal covers it if missed | G5.3, P5.4, E3.7 |
| **Note** | What the student gets for earning a discovery from a conversation or a key passage, written in the role's voice. Notes are the source of truth for what the student knows, and both kinds work the same everywhere | P7.2, P8–P8.3, P8.5, P13.2 |
| **Open question** | A hidden question linked to discoveries, surfaced only as a hint | G5.1, P7.3 |
| **Unearned assertion** | A claim the student makes without a matching note. The character questions its basis; nothing changes in the game | P8.2–P8.3 |

## Documents
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Document** | A newspaper, letter, telegram, speech, minutes or memoir extract. Real excerpts where a source exists | G10, G10.1 |
| **Provenance** | Who, when and where, shown in a form that fits the period (masthead and date, sender and recipient) | P4 |
| **Source label** | From the teacher's materials, from other cited sources, or invented. Facts without a citation count as invented | G1.2, G1.2.1, E3.4, T3 |
| **Key passage** | A marked part of a document. Selecting a passage that overlaps it creates a note | G10.3, P13.2 |

## Pacing and help
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Stuck** | No new note or stance change for a time that adapts to the time left (45 seconds to 3 minutes; 2 minutes without a timer) | P5.1–P5.1.4 |
| **Hint** | Automatic help shown as the role's own thoughts: an open question, then an angle, then a story event | P5–P5.7 |
| **Angle** | The second hint step: a more specific direction, such as a person to ask, a passage to read, a note to pass on or a document to show | P5.2, P5.2.1 |
| **Story event** | A written event fired by time, a condition, being stuck, or the timer running out. Effects: summons, movement, a document delivered, news, the defining moment beginning | G11, P14–P14.2 |
| **Final call** | The story event that brings the defining moment to the student when the timer runs out | P1.2, P9.5 |

## Decisions and endings
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Defining moment** | The scene each stage builds to, where a real figure decides based on what the student advises, conveys or keeps back | P9 |
| **Nudge** | A character's reminder, without blocking, if core discoveries are missing at the defining moment | P9.6 |
| **Outcome** | One of 3–4 written results of a defining moment, chosen by written rules with no randomness | G6, P9.3 |
| **Prediction** | One tap: what the real figure will decide, from outcomes that are neither secret nor already closed. Revealed with the reason when the next stage opens; never scored | P3–P3.6 |
| **Ending** | An outcome of the final defining moment: 1 historical, 2–3 alternative, 0–1 secret. No good or bad labels | G6.4 |
| **Closed ending** | An ending made unreachable by an earlier stage's outcome | G6.3, P9.9 |
| **Secret ending** | A plausible path history didn't take, reached through a hidden go-between act when 2–3 skilful-play conditions are met | G6.1–G6.1.2, P9.11 |

## End of game
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Epilogue** | A short scene showing how the student's run ended, labelled as their simulation | E1 |
| **The Interview** | A short in-chat conversation, essentially a gamified reflection: a question fitting the ending, a verdict, notes as evidence, one push-back, a response | E2–E2.6 |
| **Verdict** | One of 3 written answers to the Interview question | G6.5, E2.1 |
| **Push-back** | The interviewer's single counter-point, chosen by rule: the student's own contrary note, then a missed core discovery, then a general counter-argument | G6.6, E2.4–E2.4.2 |
| **Reveal** | What actually happened, where the run split from history, corrections and missed core discoveries, with more on request | E3–E3.8 |

## Teacher
| Term | Meaning | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **Class summary** | A navigable overview of the class's thinking, as totals: where students got stuck, predictions, verdicts, discoveries missed, endings, anonymised answers | T2–T2.7 |
| **Flagged message** | An abusive, hateful or provocative message, shown to the teacher per student for safeguarding | P16, T5, C9 |
